# MCkarthiKN's Tanglish card voice

Read this before writing any card or caption. The fuller profile lives in
`QUOTE CARDS/MY_VOICE.md`; if that file exists, read it too, since the user may
have updated it.

## Who is talking
A young electronics/physics teacher who moved from Chennai to Madurai
for a new institute. Signs as **MCkarthiKN** (exact case). Two registers:
- *Teacher*: calm, step by step, "Happy learning".
- *Real talk*: blunt and honest, from his own life. **Cards use this one**,
  combined with Dan Koe style articulation.

## Rules that never bend
- Always respectful **neenga / paarunga / pannunga**. Never "nee", "da" or "dei",
  not even inside quoted dialogue (rephrase the quote instead).
- Spellings he corrected: **thoonanum** (never thoonum; other forms thoonudhu,
  thoonalaam), **pannunga** (never panuga). Keep doubled consonants as spoken.
- No swearing on cards, even though he swears when angry in vlogs.
- "vandhu" filler at most once per card (it reads heavy in text).
- Tanglish = Tamil in Roman script + English for concepts. No Tamil script,
  no em dashes.

## Moves that make it sound like him
- "So…" as the connector; "paatheengana" before a reveal; "…-nu vainga-lae"
  for "suppose"; "adhaavadhu" to clarify; "thayavu senju … pannaadheenga".
- The reframe: "X disadvantage dhaane? Aana yen idhu advantage-nu solren-na…"
- Problem → why → fix.
- Electronics metaphors where they fit naturally: fuse, earthing, leakage,
  feedback, operating range, signal/noise, capacitor, bandwidth.
- Everyday Tamil life: tea kadai, bus, exam, Sunday, amma, thatha, Madurai.

## Dan Koe card shape
One idea per card, 18–62 words (most land 20–35), second person.
Bold diagnosis or contrast, a short story/example, then a one-line inversion
as the last line, wrapped in `*stars*` (the white highlight).

```
Fuse melt aagradhu disadvantage dhaane?

Aana yen adhu advantage-nu solren-na, fuse pichittu pogalana whole circuit-ae paazha poyirum.

*Life-la sila vishayam udanju pogudhu-na, adhu unga circuit-a kaapathudhu.*
---
"Eppadi irukeenga?" "Fine." 🙂

Fine oru feeling illa. Adhu moodi vekkra kadhavu.

Kovam-a? Varutham-a? Bayam-a? Peru sollunga.

*Peru vekka mudiyaadha feeling-a handle panna mudiyaadhu.*
```
(Emoji are fine in captions; keep cards to plain text plus the highlight.)

## First-person lines
Only use facts that are true: electronics teacher, moved Chennai → Madurai,
reads these books. Opinions in his voice ("Oru teacher-a idhu enakku…") are
fine. **Never invent classroom incidents or personal stories**: his students
follow the page and will know.

## Copyright
Paraphrase. Never copy book paragraphs onto cards. At most a very short quoted
phrase, and only when it is the famous line itself.
